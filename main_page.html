The big question

From information to impact.

Women’s cancers are complex, heterogeneous and deeply individual. Turning diverse patient information into meaningful clinical insights calls for a conversation across disciplines.

Our special session brings together biomedical engineers, AI researchers and clinicians to explore how imaging, longitudinal data and intelligent technologies can support more personalized cancer care.

“How can we transform multimodal patient data into actionable information for personalized care?”
What we’ll explore

Five perspectives.
One shared goal.

Follow the pathway from patient data to clinical impact, with space for discussion across disciplines.

01 /

Extract meaningful information

Quantitative imaging, biomarkers and computational approaches for understanding disease.

02 /

Connect the data

Multimodal and longitudinal integration, patient representation, fairness and robustness.

03 /

Support better decisions

Predictive and explainable AI, generative models and emerging digital twin approaches.

04 /

Start from clinical needs

Patient selection, surgical complexity, operability and personalized treatment strategies.

05 /

Translate into practice

Clinical validation, generalizability and integration into real oncology workflows.

THE CONNECTION /

Engineering meets clinical care.

Bringing complementary perspectives together to discuss what it takes to move from promising methods to meaningful tools.

Research background

Ideas grounded in research.

The session brings together research interests in AI-driven precision oncology and women’s cancer care.

OVARIAN CANCER · GENERATIVE AI

PREDICT

Predictive Response and Disease Evaluation in Ovarian Cancer with Generative AI explores imaging and data-driven approaches to disease evolution and treatment response. PREDICT is funded by Fondazione Regionale per la Ricerca Biomedica (FRRB).

MULTIMODAL AI · DECISION SUPPORT

IMPACT-OC

A proposed research initiative exploring multimodal generative AI and patient-specific modelling for decision support in ovarian cancer.
